One job is counted as one pallet. A forklift-only job can weigh more than 2500 kg or occupy more than 2.0 m3. Loading starts at departure, which is the job cutoff plus the staging buffer (30 minutes unless changed). A job waits on the half-open interval from its finish until departure. A job that finishes at or after departure is not in the count. This report is not a dock capacity.
