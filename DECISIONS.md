## 2026-10-13

- The set-partitioning chunker is `order_picking/chunker/chunk_model.py` (HiGHS through `scipy.optimize.milp`). It does not replace the savings heuristic. A source order that fits a picker stays whole. Orders at the same stop may share a job. A line or source order that fits no single picker is reported and not split. Weight and volume are tested on the same picker. A must-ship line is covered by the same equality as every other line, and only a priority-eligible job may carry it. The default priority rule is any feasible job that contains the line. `priority_max_lines` is the optional tighter cap. Forklift-only jobs set `required_equipment` to `forklift`. The time limit is 120 seconds per truck. A cover in hand is kept. No cover falls back to the savings jobs for that truck. Compare the two chunkers on the lines both keep. The formulation is `order_picking/chunker/formulation.md`. The cost of keeping an order whole is `order_picking/chunker/order_whole_cost_memo.md`.
- Stops may share a job by default, the same rule as the savings chunker. `--destination-grouping` keeps one stop per job. A fitting order stays whole either way. On the 2,247 shared lines, one stop is 195 jobs and 3,204.623 minutes. Mixing stops is 171 jobs and 2,975.885 minutes (919 s, every truck optimal, no fallback). Line-level savings is 168 jobs and 2,371.931 minutes. About 604 minutes remain with orders kept whole. A job of at most 15 lines has at most 15 pick locations, so the 16-location check adds no further rejection at that line cap.
- `--time-limit` stays 120 seconds and applies to HiGHS after the tours are priced. `solve.csv` records the relative gap. A gap of 0 is a proved optimum. A cover in hand is kept when the limit stops the proof. No cover falls back to savings and that row has no gap. A 60 second cap on truck 1 and truck 2 reproduced the proved covers, 14 jobs at 253.200 minutes and 12 jobs at 211.754 minutes, both at gap 0. HiGHS took 0.06 s and 0.07 s. The wall clock, 425 s and 115 s, is pricing the candidate tours.
- Held-Karp reads a local distance table instead of the travel dictionary on every leg. On this file the location-set cache hits only the single orders, and the cheapest-outgoing lower bound dropped no merged candidate. Numba is not used. With the table, truck 1 is 187.7 s and truck 2 is 92.6 s, same proved covers (253.200 and 211.754 minutes, gap 0). The full wave after the table is 474.75 s, the same 171 jobs and 2,975.885 minutes, every truck gap 0, no fallback.
- Closed chunker and assignment tries, including the ones that must not be repeated, are `order_picking/TRIED.md`. Tuning `--lateness-weight`, forcing every `y = 1` in one solve, dock-switch rules, and a re-chunk loop are on that list. The agreed next objective change is a two-pass release, then lateness. It is not built.
- Phase 2 keeps the release-weight objective unless a flag is set. `--lateness-weight` (default 0) subtracts tier times minutes late under earliest-deadline order on each picker. `cutoff_time` for that term is minutes from the shift start. The must-ship lateness tier is 100 and an ordinary tier is 1. The stored release weight, including 100000, is unchanged. `--prefer-jack` (default 0) is a penalty in those same units for a jack-eligible job given to a forklift. `--require-must-ship` forces those releases and can be infeasible. `--report-dir` writes utilisation, drop detail, and late jobs. Busy minutes are the phase-1 duration, not the chunker's 5-minute overhead. Each dock solve still uses the full picker file. Dock-switch rules and a re-chunk loop are not built. Scenarios live in `order_picking/scenarios/assignment.yaml`.

## 2026-10-21

- Phase 2 still defaults to one weighted objective. `--staged-assignment` releases must-ship jobs by count, then other jobs by weight, then minimises lateness, then balance only when `balance_weight` > 0, and the jack count only when `prefer_jack` > 0. Each stage locks the objective value it reached (tolerance 1e-6), not the job set. The public score is the release-weight sum. `require_must_ship` remains the hard `y = 1` path.
- The stop is a tenth of the smallest positive weight, raised above about `1e-6` times an objective upper bound when that still sits below half a job. When the noise is already larger than one job, the tenth-of-a-job target is kept. A caller `mip_gap` is stored as `gap_requested` and does not loosen the solve. SciPy 1.18 accepts `mip_rel_gap` only, so HiGHS sets `mip_rel_gap = absolute / max(objective_upper, 1)`. The balance bound is twice the sum of the shift lengths. Gurobi gets `MIPGap = 0` and `MIPGapAbs` equal to that tolerance. The must-ship count stage stops at an absolute gap of 0.5. Each stage records `stop` (`proven`, `gap_reached`, `time_limit`, or `no_solution`).
- `require_must_ship` with no feasible plan sets `is_optimal` false, `infeasible_must_ship` true, and `status_reason` `required_must_ship_jobs_cannot_all_fit`. The printed line is "Required must-ship jobs cannot all fit." An empty plan without that flag stays feasible.
- `run_docks.py` solves each depot folder, then `require_shared_crew` compares busy minutes with `shift_duration`. It does not build a joint dock model.
- The 16-truck wave was rerun on 2026-10-21 with lateness weight 1 and a 60 s stage limit, into `outputs/chunk_assign/gap_wave/`. Weighted and staged each released all 84 PACK_A jobs and all 87 PACK_B jobs, including `TRK02-J02`, `TRK14-J09`, `TRK14-J15`, `TRK16-J01`, and `TRK16-J03`. Late minutes were 0. Every stage stopped `proven` with achieved absolute gap 0. The shared-crew check passed. The weighted path remains free to drop a job when lateness costs more than its weight.
- `--prefer-jack` 0.1 was run on 2026-10-21 with `--staged-assignment`, lateness weight 1, and a 60 s stage limit, into `outputs/chunk_assign/gap_wave/staged_prefer_jack/`. On that path the flag only turns on a jack-count stage whose coefficient is 1. Release stayed 400424.4 and 700414, lateness stayed 0, and the jack count proved at 0. Twelve forklift-only jobs stayed on forklifts. The returned plan keeps each dock inside 480 minutes and books PJ04 to 506.5 and PJ08 to 504.7 across both docks, so the shared-shift check raises. The default stays 0.
- `--withhold-if-late-at-start` (default off) removes a job whose duration exceeds `A_r(T)` for every eligible picker. The reason is `cannot_meet_cutoff`. Those jobs are not forced by `require_must_ship`.
- `require_shared_crew` sums `busy_minutes` across dock utilisation files and raises when a picker is past the shift. Tolerance is 0.0001 minute. A pass is that minute total. Clock overlap and the walk between docks stay outside the screen. The pass line says so. It does not build a joint dock model.
- The savings chunker raises when `max_job_volume` is set and a line has no volume. The job cap stays the heaviest picker.
- A chunker cutoff of 30 is minutes (departure minus the 30-minute staging buffer). `deadline_minutes` reads that number as minutes. A stored `priority_weight` is kept.
